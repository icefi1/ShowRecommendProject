The problem

Streaming catalogues don’t have the words.

The source catalogue offers 15 genres for television. Horror isn’t one of them. Neither is romance, thriller, or history.

What the catalogue can say

DramaMystery Sci-Fi & Fantasy HorrorRomance ThrillerHistory

85 shows in the catalogue are about romance. None can be labelled “romance.” The vocabulary simply doesn’t exist.

What we score instead

horrorcreepy jumpscaresslow burn plot complexityromance bleakcosy campy+28 more

Not labels a show either has or doesn’t. Scores from 0 to 1, because “a bit creepy” and “relentlessly creepy” are different requests.

Why it matters

Named axes can explain themselves.

Because every dimension has a name, every recommendation comes with a reason — generated from the same numbers that produced the ranking, so it can never contradict them.

Sweet Home — shares monster, supernatural horror; but is shorter, far less complex, and much gorier.
Broadchurch — shares small town, investigation; but is far less frightening and more evenly paced.

No “because you watched…”. An actual answer.

Steering

Turn one thing up.
Turn another down.

Found something almost right? Move a single axis. Keep the dread, drop the gore. Keep the setting, lose the twenty-season commitment. The catalogue re-ranks in under a millisecond.
